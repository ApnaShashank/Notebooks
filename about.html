About

"Hello! Welcome to 'OUR NOTES'. This website is built to help everyone who is taking their first step into the world of programming. Our goal is to present complex concepts of languages like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python in the form of simple and easy notes, so that you can learn quickly and effectively."

"Hi, I'm Shashank, the creator of this website. When I was learning to code, I often felt the need for short and straightforward notes. I started 'OUR NOTES' to fill this exact gap. It's a platform where students and new developers can find quick and precise information on any topic. All the notes here are written in very simple language so that anyone can understand them easily. I hope this website proves to be a helpful friend on your coding journey. Happy Coding!"
